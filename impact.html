Why Skipping the Stuff Works

1. Cutting Waste at the Source

Most takeout is eaten at home or work where reusable utensils are already on hand. Unrequested plastic forks and sauce packets go straight from the bag to the landfill.

2. Direct Savings for Local Businesses

Supply costs continue to climb. Providing disposable accessories only upon request lets restaurants significantly trim non-food inventory expenses.

3. Cleaner Local Parks & Waterways

Fewer single-use items mean less neighborhood litter blowing into local parks, storm drains, and municipal waste streams.
