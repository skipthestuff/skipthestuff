Why "Skip the Stuff" Matters

1. Cutting Waste at the Source

Most takeout food is consumed at home or at work where reusable utensils are already available. Unrequested plastic forks and sauce packets go straight from the bag to the trash.

2. Supporting Local Restaurants

Supply costs continue to rise. By providing disposable items only upon request, restaurants significantly lower inventory spending on non-food items.

3. Cleaner Neighborhoods

Fewer single-use plastics mean less litter in our local parks, waterways, and municipal waste streams.
