Simple Choices. Cleaner Communities.

A volunteer-led initiative reducing single-use plastic waste by making takeout accessories optional across local restaurants.

Take the Pledge
🍽️

For Restaurants

Save money on packaging costs and reduce waste. Join our by-request pledge to make cutlery and condiments optional.

🛍️

For Individuals

Politely request "no plastics" when ordering takeout. Small everyday actions create massive reductions in local landfill waste.

🌱

Community Impact

Millions of unopened plastic forks and ketchup packets end up in landfills daily. Together, we can change the default.
