Skip the Stuff

A volunteer-led initiative reducing single-use plastic waste by making takeout accessories optional.

Simple Choices, Cleaner Communities

Millions of plastic forks, spoons, napkins, and condiment packets are thrown away unopened every single day. Skip the Stuff empowers restaurants and consumers to cut down on unnecessary waste simply by asking first.
