Take Action as a Diner

Every single takeout order is an opportunity to reduce plastic pollution in our community.

📝

1. Leave a Note

When ordering online or through delivery apps, write "No plastic cutlery, napkins, or sauce packets, please!" in the order instructions.

🗣️

2. Speak Up

When ordering in person or over the phone, explicitly let staff know you don't need disposable utensils before they bag your food.

📣

3. Spread the Word

Encourage your favorite local spots to adopt a by-request policy and share our community initiative with friends and family.
