Take Action as a Diner

Every takeout order is an opportunity to reduce plastic pollution in our community.

📝

1. Leave Order Notes

When ordering online or via food delivery apps, type a polite note in the special instructions field: "No plastic utensils, extra napkins, or condiment packets, please!"

🗣️

2. Speak Up at Pickup

When ordering in person or over the phone, explicitly state your preference before they pack your order: "I have utensils at home, so please hold the plastics."

📣

3. Advocate Locally

Encourage your favorite neighborhood spots to transition to a by-request policy. Highlighting that it saves them money on packaging supplies makes it an easy win for them.

How to Talk to Your Favorite Restaurants

Most restaurant managers want to reduce waste—and packaging overhead—but simply stick with what's familiar. A friendly conversation can nudge them toward a by-request policy.

💡 Simple Conversation Starter

"I love ordering food from here! Have you ever considered making cutlery and sauce packets by-request only? It saves so much plastic waste from going into our landfills, and it cuts down on your supply costs."

🎯 Key Points to Mention

    Cost Savings: Single-use items add up fast in monthly packaging supply costs. Customer Goodwill: Diners appreciate businesses taking proactive steps to cut waste. Free Community Partner Listing: Point them to skipthestuff.org so they can take the pledge and get recognized locally.
