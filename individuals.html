Take Action as a Diner

Every takeout order is an opportunity to reduce plastic pollution in our community.

📝

1. Leave Order Notes

When ordering online or via delivery apps, write in the notes: "No plastic utensils, extra napkins, or sauce packets, please!"

🗣️

2. Speak Up at Pickup

When ordering in person or over the phone, state your preference before packing: "I have utensils at home, so please hold the plastics."

📣

3. Advocate Locally

Encourage your favorite neighborhood spots to transition to a by-request policy using our sample letter and advocacy tips below.

Get Involved: Write to Your Favorite Restaurant

Most owners want to save money on supply overhead and cut waste, but need a gentle nudge to make cutlery optional. Copy the template below and email or drop it off at your go-to spot!

Subject: A simple cost-saving idea for [Restaurant Name]

Dear [Restaurant Owner/Manager Name],

I am a regular customer and love dining at [Restaurant Name]! I’m writing to ask if you would consider adopting a "by-request" policy for single-use plastic utensils, extra napkins, and condiment packets on takeout orders.

Making disposable items available only when explicitly requested by customers helps reduce unnecessary plastic landfill waste in our community. Better yet, it directly cuts down on your monthly packaging and supply costs.

Our local community initiative, Skip the Stuff (skipthestuff.org), helps independent restaurants make this transition smoothly and lists participating businesses on our directory.

Thank you so much for your delicious food and for considering this small change to support a cleaner community!

Best regards,

[Your Name]
[Your Neighborhood / Town]
